How FocusShield Works

1. Major Search & LMS Sync

Select or search your major (CS, Medical, Eng, BBA) on the Dashboard to auto-sync your course deadlines and customized study verification gates.

2. Friction Gate & 5-Hour Lock

Tapping a protected app forces you to state your intent. If time runs out, a strict 5-Hour Hard Lock is enforced to protect your focus.

3. Namaz & Rest Routine Guide

Prayer Slots: During Namaz times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), non-essential apps pause so you can pray without distraction.
Rest Slot: Scheduled break window (e.g., Dhuhr & Lunch) where app restrictions pause automatically for healthy rest.

4. Emergency Family Calls

Emergency contacts (Parents, Siblings) bypass all hard locks so family can always reach you.
